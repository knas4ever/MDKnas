# MDKnas Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

MDKnas is a markdown editor that runs entirely on your computer.

## What we collect

Nothing. MDKnas does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no telemetry and no advertising.

## Your files

The files you open, edit and save stay on your device. MDKnas reads and writes them only when you ask it to. They are never uploaded anywhere.

## Settings

Your preferences (theme, font size, autosave interval and the last folder you opened) are saved in a small settings file on your device. You can delete it at any time.

## Network access

MDKnas does not connect to the internet on its own. It only opens a link in your default browser when you click one.

## Microsoft components

MDKnas displays its interface with the Microsoft Edge WebView2 runtime, which is part of Windows. Microsoft's own privacy statement covers that component, and it is outside the control of MDKnas. The app is distributed through the Microsoft Store.

## Children

MDKnas does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
